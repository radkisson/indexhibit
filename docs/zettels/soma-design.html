Soma Design

2026-08-18 · zettel

Design that starts from the lived body rather than from the screen: the body is the interface's ground, not its mount. In practice this means the design question shifts from "what does the user see" to "where is the body and what does it know".

Bodies of Water — Safetyballing on the Lake is the soma-design piece: the body sealed in water, resonance as the instrument. Boardmusic — What the Water Taught Me approaches the same ground from performance: body schema extended by paddleboard and lake.
