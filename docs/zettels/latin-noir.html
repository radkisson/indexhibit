Latin Noir

2026-08-18 · zettel

The public shorthand for Saramago: Latin Noir. intimate. It names the style instead of explaining the person — declaration, not audience-targeting. Bolero containment, dramatic without irony; humor that protects vulnerability rather than deflecting it.

Doctrine: references are permissions, not restrictions — take the permission, leave the costume. The wound stays intimate and real; the band can grow but the distance cannot. See Saramago — ESP-001, grounded in Nueva Canción Lineage.
