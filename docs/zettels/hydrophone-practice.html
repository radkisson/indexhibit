Hydrophone Practice

2026-08-18 · zettel

The hydrophone turns water from scenery into medium. Placing it outside the body's protection (outside a swimming helmet, against the skin of a sphere) makes the body's own resonance in water audible — water as instrument, not backdrop.

This is the "three waters" construction in Bodies of Water — Safetyballing on the Lake: the lake's water, the body's water, the synthesized water. The hydraulophone in Boardmusic — What the Water Taught Me is the same lineage made playable: an instrument that requires water to function at all.
