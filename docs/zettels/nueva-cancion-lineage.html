Nueva Canción Lineage

2026-08-18 · zettel

I was trained in symphonic choir repertoire rooted in nueva canción — the Chilean song tradition where political song and folk material meet art music. Carmina Burana next to Violeta Parra's descendants: that tension is the inheritance.

The lineage runs forward through my choral history into the present: Performance Practice — Choir to Now carries the voice, and Latin Noir is what it becomes when the choir empties out and one voice remains — the stripped test that every Saramago — ESP-001 song must survive.
