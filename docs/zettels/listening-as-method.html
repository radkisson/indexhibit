Listening as Method

2026-08-18 · zettel

Listening is not reception; it is a discipline with technique. The ear can be trained the way the hand is trained — and most of my works are devices for training it: making water, rooms, bodies, or data audible so they can be attended to.

See the practice statement in Bodies of Water — Safetyballing on the Lake, the environmental reading in Boardmusic — What the Water Taught Me, and the sonification work Ball → Sound — Sonifying a Translucent Ball.
