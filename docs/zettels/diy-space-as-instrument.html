DIY Space as Instrument

2026-08-18 · zettel

A self-managed cultural space (RadStorm, OndaSonora, CAFCA) is an instrument: it must be built, tuned, maintained, and played. Sustainability is not admin work appended to the art — it is the craft of keeping the instrument playable. This is why an engineering thesis on stakeholder management RadStorm & DIY Infrastructure counts as art practice, and why building a stompbox from KiCad netlists Zynthian Stompbox — Neural Multi-FX Floor Unit does too.

Related method: Listening as Method — a space is heard before it is used.
