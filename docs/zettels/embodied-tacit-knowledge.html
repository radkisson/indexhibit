Embodied Tacit Knowledge

2026-08-18 · zettel

The performer’s body holds knowledge that resists formalization. This is not ignorance or mystery; it is the seam where soma design meets the tacit-knowledge research program. Goranzon’s skill theory (knowing-in-action that cannot be fully articulated) applied to the working musician whose fingers find the voicing before the mind names it. AI systems that model musical decision-making from audio or score data capture the output of this knowledge but not its embodiment: the coupling of body, instrument, room, and intention that produces a phrase in real time.

See Soma Design, Listening as Method, and the research in Conocimiento tácito y cognición corporizada en la música.
