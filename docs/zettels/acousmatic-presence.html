Acousmatic Presence

2026-08-18 · ZETTEL

The productive tension between visible performer and hidden sound source is not a failure of the acousmatic tradition but its original condition. Pythagoras's students listened behind a curtain: the practice was always about the relationship between presence and absence of source. In live performance, placing the sound source behind the audience while the performer stands in silence stages this tension explicitly. The oxymoron of live acousmatics is actually the fertile edge where the tradition returns to its roots.

See Listening as Method, Hydrophone Practice, and the scene mapping in Acústica chilena: panorama 2020-2026.
