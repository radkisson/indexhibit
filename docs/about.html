About

I'm Rhyzome Adkisson-Rivas, a sound artist and researcher working between Toronto and Concepción.

My practice treats listening as a technical and bodily discipline. I build instruments, infrastructures, and recordings that make hidden sound legible: water systems, room resonance, the body as a resonant medium.

I trained as an industrial engineer at Universidad de Concepción and as a classical vocalist, and I now study jazz at Humber College while researching water-and-sound interfaces with Steve Mann's group at the University of Toronto. Before returning to academia I spent a decade building DIY cultural infrastructure in Chile and Canada — a crowdfunding platform, an arts education nonprofit, a community studio — which I treat as the same practice: making the conditions under which sound can happen.

This site is the index: finished works, research in progress, and a growing note graph. Music releases live at Espectro.
