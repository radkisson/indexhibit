Bodies of Water — Safetyballing on the Lake

2026-08-07 · EXHIBIT

About

A soma-design piece for one baller and a lake. A performer sealed inside a translucent sphere roughly two metres across, floated on an urban lake, sounded through three waters at once: the lake's own (via hydrophone), the body's own (voice and breath), and a synthesized water driven from shore.

Apparatus

The shore station — the hydromancer — is a luminance-blob tracker that reads the sphere's drift across the lake and drives the synthesized layer from it. A live hydraulophone (the water-instrument invented by Steve Mann) sounds at the water's edge, carrying the water-instrument lineage into the piece. The sphere's glow and its drift read together as the light-swim.

Conceptual ground

Where Tangible Bits made the digital graspable, here water holds you: the interface circumscribes the body. The reading follows Voegelin's sonic fiction — sound as generative, not documentary.

Status

Full paper under review at TEI 2027 (Tangible, Embedded & Embodied Interaction), Lisboa, 24–27 January 2027, theme "The Body in Space". Venue-site anonymized for double-blind review; site details restore at camera-ready.
