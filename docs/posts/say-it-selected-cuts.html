SAY IT: Selected Cuts

2026 · EXHIBIT

About

SAY IT: Selected Cuts is a publishing project under Espectro. It is an archive of devastating lines, presented in forensic, catalogued format. Each entry captures a single utterance that lands before the listener sees it coming.

The Archive

Every entry in the book follows the same structure:

  • The line itself, verbatim.
  • Source: the film, song, conversation, or book where it lives.
  • Speaker: who said it.
  • Target: who or what the line cuts toward.
  • Use when: the emotional or performative context where deploying this line would land.

The result reads like a field manual for language that does damage. Not a quote book. A weaponized anthology where each entry is selected for maximum emotional density per word.

The Book as Object

A6 pocket format. Matte black cover. One hundred entries. Designed to be carried, pulled out at a bar, read in one sitting or flipped to a random page when the moment demands it.

The physicality matters. A6 fits in a jacket pocket. The matte black cover makes it look like something you found, not something you bought.

Strategy

Instagram-first: individual entries post as image-text slides, building an audience before print. Each slide is one entry. The feed becomes a serialized version of the archive.

Self-publish: 200-500 copies. The curation practice is the art. The book is the artifact of that practice.
