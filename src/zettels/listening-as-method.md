title: Listening as Method
date: 2026-08-18
---

Listening is not reception; it is a discipline with technique. The ear can be trained the way the hand is trained — and most of my works are devices for training it: making water, rooms, bodies, or data audible so they can be attended to.

See the practice statement in [[safetyballing]], the environmental reading in [[boardmusic]], and the sonification work [[ball-tracking-sonification]].
