---
title: Sonic Testimony
date: 2026-08-18
---

Art is vibration that enters the body before meaning arrives, and what it creates is not an audience but witnesses. This reframes the aesthetic encounter from reception (decoding a message) to somatic event (being entered by sound). Tolstoy's infection theory, Korsmeyer's sensory hierarchy, and Sontag's anti-interpretation converge here: the work's primary channel is not semantic but vibrational. A singer whose body rotation becomes sound in real time is not expressing an idea but discovering what the body already knows.
