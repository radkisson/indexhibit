---
title: Radiological Literacy
date: 2026-08-18
---

A measurement is not a number; it is a number plus a definition. 792 Bq/kg counting only parent isotopes and 792 Bq/kg presented as chain-total activity are two different claims wearing the same figure. Public safety language that omits the definition is not lying and not safe — it is undefined.

This is the whole argument of [[la-cadena-que-falta]], and it generalizes: the discipline of asking "what is being counted, what is excluded, what was assumed" is the same discipline as [[listening-as-method]] — attention to what a signal actually carries before trusting what it seems to say.
