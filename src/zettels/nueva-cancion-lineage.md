date: 2026-08-18

I was trained in symphonic choir repertoire rooted in nueva canción — the Chilean song tradition where political song and folk material meet art music. Carmina Burana next to Violeta Parra's descendants: that tension is the inheritance.

The lineage runs forward through my choral history into the present: [[performance-practice]] carries the voice, and [[latin-noir]] is what it becomes when the choir empties out and one voice remains — the stripped test that every [[saramago-esp-001]] song must survive.
