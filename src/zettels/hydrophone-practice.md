---
title: Hydrophone Practice
date: 2026-08-18
---

The hydrophone turns water from scenery into medium. Placing it outside the body's protection (outside a swimming helmet, against the skin of a sphere) makes the body's own resonance in water audible — water as instrument, not backdrop.

This is the "three waters" construction in [[safetyballing]]: the lake's water, the body's water, the synthesized water. The hydraulophone in [[boardmusic]] is the same lineage made playable: an instrument that requires water to function at all.
