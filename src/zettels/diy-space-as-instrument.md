---
title: DIY Space as Instrument
date: 2026-08-18
---

A self-managed cultural space (RadStorm, OndaSonora, CAFCA) is an instrument: it must be built, tuned, maintained, and played. Sustainability is not admin work appended to the art — it is the craft of keeping the instrument playable. This is why an engineering thesis on stakeholder management [[radstorm-diy-infrastructure]] counts as art practice, and why building a stompbox from KiCad netlists [[zynthian-stompbox]] does too.

Related method: [[listening-as-method]] — a space is heard before it is used.
