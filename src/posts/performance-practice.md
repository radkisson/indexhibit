## About

I grew up in Concepción singing symphonic choir repertoire rooted in nueva canción — Carmina Burana, La Traviata, Die Zauberflöte. A FONDART scholarship funded my classical voice training in 2016. In 2025 I performed at the World Series opening ceremony inside Rogers Centre.

## Now

Night Rush: 80s and hair metal covers in Toronto. Jazz study at Humber College. The current live set is *Saramago y La Fiebre* — bolero noir, jazz huachaca y manouche rock de puerto. Live-work orbit includes TSONAMI (Valparaíso sound art), with the project *Murciélagos: contactos y especies* in development, and a nine-market international research file (WOMEX, BIME, CIRCULART, jazzahead!, …).

## System

The repertoire is run as a system: a 46-song vocal reference, a master repertoire database, setlists built per audience and room.
