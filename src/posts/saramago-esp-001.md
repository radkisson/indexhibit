## About

Saramago is my singer-songwriter project: piano-driven, harmonically literate songs in Spanish, written and produced in Toronto. Voice and harmony are the column; piano and guitar are vehicles. The reference space is Fito Páez — melody that rewards attention, chords that surprise without showing off, intimate without being fragile.

Public shorthand: *Latin Noir. intimate.* — voice / guitar / sax.

## Release

Debut catalog number **ESP-001** on [Espectro](https://espectro.fm). Songs in Spanish; the record is built around the words.

## Live format

*Saramago y La Fiebre* — bolero noir, jazz huachaca y manouche rock de puerto.

## Rules that hold

No autotune as a stylistic choice. A song must survive being stripped back to voice and piano. The wound stays intimate and real.
