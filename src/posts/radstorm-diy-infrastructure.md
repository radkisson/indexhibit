## About

A decade of building and sustaining places where sound happens, in Chile and Canada — treated here as one practice.

## The organizations

- **OndaSonora** (Concepción, 2016–2018) — co-founder. Crowdfunding for Chilean artists; $40,000 raised; accepted to pitch at KTH Stockholm.
- **CAFCA** (Concepción, 2016–2020) — co-founder. Arts education nonprofit; 300+ students over four years.
- **RadStorm Arts Collective** (Halifax, 2022–2023) — program & studio coordinator. Community studio: equipment, workshops, $10k+ in grants secured.

## The thesis

*Enhancing long-term sustainability of Halifax's Radstorm artist collective through stakeholder management strategies* — Ingeniería Industrial, Universidad de Concepción (memoria, 2023–2024). Mixed methods: stakeholder interviews against the collective's operational record, drawing on ~55 sources in DIY cultures and organizational sustainability.
